Before work begins

Learn what the electrical system is before you commit.

Consider a detached house with an attached garage. The cable route from the panel to the garage can matter, and garage lights or receptacles may sit on circuits shared with rooms in the house, so ask the seller what each one connects to.

Whether you are weighing an offer or have just taken possession, gather the seller disclosures, the inspection report and any earlier ESA paperwork first. An electrical review complements a home inspection and never replaces it.

What should I find out about the wiring before making an offer?

Start with the home inspection report, the seller disclosures and any earlier ESA paperwork. Then list the questions those documents leave open, so a Licensed Electrical Contractor can address each one in a written scope.

Does an electrical review replace the home inspection report?

No. A home inspection covers the whole house at a general level, while an electrical review looks at defined equipment in more detail. Treat the two as complementary and keep both documents together for later reference.

How do I compare two quotes written for different scopes?

Put the scopes side by side before looking at the prices. Check which equipment, areas and tests each one includes, and whether it relies on the inspection report or on its own visit. Ask each contractor to state what is left out.

Which electrical items should I raise with the seller or lawyer?

Raise anything the documents or the inspection flagged: visible defects, unfinished work and missing ESA paperwork. A lawyer or the seller can advise on the sale terms, while the contractor explains the technical scope.

Does sending the form commit me to anything with this site?

No. The site gives planning guidance, and an enquiry sent through it is received and reviewed, but the site sets up no visits, quotes no prices and performs no electrical work. Before you agree to anything, a provider or Licensed Electrical Contractor separately confirms its identity and licence, then settles the job, price and dates with you.
